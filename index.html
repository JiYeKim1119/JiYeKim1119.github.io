About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Communication at the University of Southern California. My research examines toxic and prosocial play in online multiplayer games as multidimensional social behaviors, with a focus on relationship selection and social influence.

I use surveys, network and behavioral data, social network analysis, machine learning, and computational text analysis to study communication processes in online game environments.

  • Game Studies
  • Media Psychology
  • Social Network Analysis
  • Computational Methods
  • Natural Language Processing

Dissertation: Toxic and Prosocial Gameplay in Online Games: Relationship Selection and Socially Driven Behavior

Adviser: Dmitri Williams. Committee: Dmitri Williams (Chair), Aimei Yang, and Lindsay Young.
