Understanding social interaction in digital environments.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Communication at the University of Southern California. My research examines how interactions in online games and adjacent digital platforms shape relationships, psychological needs, social influence, and behavior. I combine communication theory with surveys, large-scale behavioral trace data, social network analysis, and computational methods.

Research

My research program focuses on the social and psychological processes that emerge when people interact within algorithmically organized digital spaces.

01

Social Interaction in Online Games

I examine how cooperation, competition, repeated encounters, group membership, and interpersonal exposure affect player experiences and behavior.

02

Algorithms as Interaction Infrastructure

I study how matchmaking and other platform systems organize social exposure, shape influence, and facilitate or constrain relationship formation.

03

Computational Communication

I use behavioral logs, surveys, social network analysis, machine learning, and text-based methods to measure communication processes at scale.
